One sentence: Keyhalt stops a rogue or compromised admin agent fleet from acting by freezing its keys, and it proves it on chain every time.

Audience: NEAR Hackathon judges, module reviewers, SRE teams running agent fleets on NEAR.

Duration: about 78 seconds. 1280 x 720, 30 fps. Silent with captions, in the way the X feed is consumed.

Assets (all real, exported from this repository):

- the claim ids and test counts: from `CLAIMS.md` (41 rows, 42 with the correction note)
- the testnet accounts used in the canonical run: `fleet-v03-5cff9e99.testnet`
- tx hashes from `evidence/testnet-v03/run.json`
